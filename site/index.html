How it works

Sign in with your provider. Your browser opens, you approve, the token stays on your Mac. Yon has no server and never sees your password.

Files stream in pieces. Open a 40 GB video and it plays in about a second, because only the part being watched is fetched.

Nothing else to install. No kernel extension, no driver. Yon mounts the drive the way macOS already mounts network volumes. Needs macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon or Intel.

Your storage, not ours

Yon is free. Space comes from the plan you already pay for: Google gives 15 GB free and 2 TB for about $10 a month. Yon adds no bill and holds no copy of your files.

Works with anything rclone speaks: Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, Cloudflare R2, Backblaze B2, Amazon S3, and dozens more.

A Yon drive is a normal folder at ~/Yon. Claude Code, Cursor, grep and Final Cut read and write it like any other path, without downloading whole files first.
